How the forecast works
  • A linear regression predicts the mortgage rate from one input: the fed funds rate, the midpoint of the Federal Reserve's target range. It's fit on monthly data since September 2016.
  • The fed funds path runs in straight lines from today's rate through each year-end projection in the Fed's Summary of Economic Projections (midpoint of the central tendency).
  • The shaded band is a 95% prediction interval for each month.
  • The model is tested with five-fold time-series cross-validation. Each fold trains on earlier months and tests on the months after them.
